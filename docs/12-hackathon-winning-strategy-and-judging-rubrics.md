# 12 - Hackathon Winning Strategy & Judging Rubrics

## Target Track: The Taskmaster ($20,000 Category Prize + $50,000 Grand Prize)

The Taskmaster track demands: *"Build a complete workflow, not just a chatbot. Don't just make an agent that writes text. Make one that takes action. Find a messy, multi-step chore... handles the details, sends the right info to the right places, and proves it can do the heavy lifting for you."*

---

## 1. Rubric Scorecard & Strategic Proof Points

### Criterion 1: Innovation & Operational Utility (40% Weight)
- **What Judges Look For**: How much real-world friction does the agent remove on its own? Rewarding autonomous, high-value action over simple chat loops.
- **RoomieOps Proof Points**:
  - Eliminates 100% of awkward human follow-ups.
  - Asynchronous background monitoring: Cloud Scheduler + PubSub fires unattended hourly pulses.
  - Multi-stage adaptive tone escalation (Friendly $\rightarrow$ Nudge $\rightarrow$ Firm $\rightarrow$ Public Flag).
  - Time-travel simulator in demo UI visually proves autonomous operations across multiple simulated days.

### Criterion 2: Architectural Discipline & Tech Stack (30% Weight)
- **What Judges Look For**: Decoupled systems, state management, security, and production-minded engineering.
- **RoomieOps Proof Points**:
  - Deep Modules with clean seams following Ousterhout principles.
  - Mathematical Min-Cash-Flow debt simplification with formal graph reduction proof.
  - Firestore dual adapter (Native Cloud Firestore + in-memory fallback).
  - Clean 3-developer FDD boundary separation and OpenAPI single source of truth.

### Criterion 3: Demo & Production Readiness (30% Weight)
- **What Judges Look For**: Live, unedited demo, clean architecture diagram, reproducible setup, visible proof running on Google Cloud.
- **RoomieOps Proof Points**:
  - Live Next.js glassmorphism web dashboard.
  - Working Gemini Multimodal receipt dropzone with live extraction.
  - One-tap UPI deep links and QR codes.
  - Proof of Google Cloud Run container deployment and Cloud Scheduler jobs.
