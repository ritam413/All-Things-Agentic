The Human Chore

The Passive Ledger Trap: Calculation != Collection

1. Manual Data Entry Friction

Roommates hate manually typing OCR amounts, invoice numbers, and line items after long workdays.

2. Room-Size & Itemized Inequity

Splitting rent or AC power equally is unfair when room sizes vary from 120 sq ft to 300 sq ft.

3. Emotional Nagging & Social Friction

Asking friends for money creates awkward tension. Chronic late payers exploit social politeness.

Why Passive Apps Fail

Existing tools are passive calculators. They compute IOUs but leave 100% of the emotional labor, reminders, follow-ups, and debt settlement to human memory.

RoomieOps AI flips the paradigm into an active background operator that executes the entire lifecycle autonomously.
